# Chapter 1 — Introduction

## Initiator Question

How can we identify the exact cell types responsible for a disease if tissues contain millions of heterogeneous cells?

---

## Introduction to Single-Cell Transcriptomics

Single-cell RNA sequencing allows measurement of gene expression at the resolution of individual cells.
